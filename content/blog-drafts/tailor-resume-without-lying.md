A tailored resume is the same career, told with a different emphasis. You aren't changing what happened. You're choosing what a busy reader sees first.

## A five-step process

1. **Read the posting and mark what repeats.** The skills mentioned several times are usually what the team cares about most.
2. **Match their language where it's true.** If they say "customer success" and you wrote "client support" for the same work, use their phrase.
3. **Reorder your bullets.** Put the most relevant achievement first under each role.
4. **Cut what doesn't help.** Removing unrelated detail makes the relevant parts stand out.
5. **Update your summary** so its first line speaks to this kind of role.

## Where the line is

**Fine:** describing a real project with the employer's terminology, or leading with your SQL work when you've done plenty of it.

**Not fine:** listing a tool you used once as a core skill, inflating your title, or changing dates. These can come out in an interview or a background check, and being caught costs far more than the missing keyword would have.

A useful test: could you talk about this line for two minutes if asked? If not, remove it.

## Doing this at volume

Hand-tailoring is worth it for roles you really want. It's hard to sustain for all of them. Iris offers three settings: off sends your resume as uploaded, honest emphasises relevant experience, and aggressive stretches further to match. We recommend honest for most people. Whichever you choose, review what your profile says, since a tool can only work from what you've given it.

For the keyword side, see [how to use resume keywords](/blog/resume-keywords-how-to-find-and-use-them/).

## Frequently asked questions

### How many versions of my resume should I keep?

Keep one complete master version, plus a focused version for each type of role you're targeting. Most people need two or three.

### Is using AI to tailor a resume acceptable?

Yes, as long as you check the result and everything in it is true. You are responsible for what you submit.
