Before you connect any tool to a job platform account, it's worth understanding what you could be risking. This is general information, not legal advice, and platform terms change, so read the current versions.

## What the platforms say

LinkedIn's User Agreement restricts the use of bots and other automated methods on its service, including third-party software that automates activity. Indeed publishes its own terms covering automated access. If a tool logs into your account and clicks on your behalf, you carry the risk of any enforcement on that account.

## What's actually at risk

The main thing to weigh is your account. For many people a LinkedIn profile represents years of connections and is how recruiters reach them. Ask yourself whether saving some application time is worth risking that.

## Questions to ask about any tool

- Does it need my LinkedIn or Indeed password or a browser session?
- Does it run inside my logged-in account?
- Where does it actually submit the application?
- What does it do if a form asks something it doesn't know?

## Applying on the source instead

Many companies take applications directly through career sites built on systems like Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby and Workday. Applying there doesn't involve your social or job-board account.

## Frequently asked questions

### Will LinkedIn ban me for using an auto-apply extension?

We can't predict any specific outcome. LinkedIn's terms restrict automation, so using such a tool on your account carries risk. Review the terms and decide for yourself.

### Is Easy Apply the same thing as automation?

No. Easy Apply is LinkedIn's own feature, used by you. For how it compares with applying on a company site, see [Easy Apply vs the company website](/blog/easy-apply-vs-company-website/).
