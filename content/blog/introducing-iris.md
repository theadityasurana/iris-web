Most people don't hate looking for a job. They hate *applying* for one.

Finding a role that excites you takes a minute. Then comes the part nobody talks about: creating yet another account, re-typing your work history into boxes that already exist in your resume, answering the same screening questions in slightly different words, and hitting submit without knowing if a human will read it. Do that thirty times and the excitement is gone.

We built Iris to remove that part entirely.

## What is Iris?

**Iris is an AI job application app that lets you apply to jobs by swiping.** It's often described as "Tinder for jobs": instead of filling in application forms, you browse job cards and swipe right on the ones you want. Iris then completes the application for you using your resume and profile.

Iris is available on the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/nextquark/id6761863089) and [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=app.rork.hireswipe_v3_clone_ceuofke).

## How does applying with a swipe work?

Iris shows you one job at a time, as a card. Each card has what you need to decide: the company, the role, the level, where it's based and the skills it asks for. Tap **Check FIT Score** to see how well it matches your profile.

- **Swipe right** to apply. Iris takes your resume and profile and completes the application for you.
- **Swipe left** to pass.
- **Swipe up** to save a role for later.

There's no separate "apply" flow, no forms and no copy-pasting. A right swipe *is* the application.

## What happens after you swipe right?

Behind every right swipe, Iris does the work you'd otherwise do by hand:

1. It reads the job description to understand what the company is asking for.
2. It fills in the application with details from your resume and profile, so your answers are consistent everywhere you apply.
3. It submits the application and adds it to your pipeline in the app, so you can see where everything stands.

When recruiters reply, their emails land against the right application inside Iris, so you never have to dig through your inbox to work out which "Software Engineer" role a message is about.

## How does Iris choose which jobs to show you?

When you sign up, you upload your resume once. Iris reads it, fills in your profile, and uses it to build your feed around the roles you're looking for, at your level. That way you spend your swipes on jobs you'd actually take. If your resume needs work first, read [how to write an ATS-friendly resume](/blog/ats-friendly-resume/).

## Is Iris free?

Yes, you can start for free, with a set of free applications every month. Invite a friend with your referral code and you both get extra swipes. Paid plans add more applications per month and features like resume tailoring for each role and automatic applying on your behalf.

Job hunting will always take some effort. It shouldn't take your evenings and weekends. That's the whole idea behind Iris.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Iris really "Tinder for jobs"?

Yes. Iris uses the same swipe interaction: swipe right on a job to apply, left to pass, and up to save it for later.

### Do I have to fill in application forms in Iris?

No. Iris completes the application for you using the resume and profile you set up once when you sign up.

### Which phones does Iris work on?

Iris is available for iPhone on the App Store and for Android on Google Play.

### Can I see how well I match a job before applying?

Yes. Tap Check FIT Score on any job card to see how well the role matches your profile before you swipe.
