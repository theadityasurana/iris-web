This Privacy Policy applies to all users of the Service worldwide. If you do not agree with any part of this Privacy Policy, you should discontinue use of the Service immediately. We encourage you to read this document in its entirety and to revisit it periodically, as it may be updated from time to time.

2. Definitions and Interpretation

“Personal Data” means any information that relates to an identified or identifiable natural person. This includes, but is not limited to, names, email addresses, phone numbers, device identifiers, IP addresses, employment history, educational records, and any other information that can be used directly or indirectly to identify you.

“Processing” means any operation or set of operations performed on Personal Data, whether or not by automated means, including collection, recording, organization, structuring, storage, adaptation, alteration, retrieval, consultation, use, disclosure by transmission, dissemination, alignment, combination, restriction, erasure, or destruction.

“Data Controller” refers to NextQuark, which determines the purposes and means of processing Personal Data collected through the Service.

“Data Processor” refers to any third party that processes Personal Data on behalf of NextQuark, such as cloud hosting providers or analytics services.

“Data Principal” refers to you, the individual whose Personal Data is being processed, as defined under India’s DPDP Act.

“Consent” means any freely given, specific, informed, and unambiguous indication of your wishes by which you, by a clear affirmative action, signify agreement to the processing of your Personal Data.

“Third-Party Job Portal” means any external career website, recruitment platform, or employer application portal that NextQuark interacts with on your behalf to assist in completing job applications.

3. Information We Collect

3.1 Information You Provide Directly

3.1.1 Account and Identity Information

When you create an account or register with the Service, we collect personal identifiers necessary to establish and maintain your account. This includes your full name, email address, phone number, and login credentials (username and password or authentication tokens from third-party sign-in providers such as Apple Sign-In or Google Sign-In). If you choose to sign in using Apple Sign-In, we may receive a unique, anonymized email address generated by Apple’s Hide My Email feature, along with your name (if you choose to share it). If you sign in using Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo URL as authorized by your Google account settings.

3.1.2 Professional and Career Profile Data

To support job application workflows, we collect and process a wide range of professional and career-related information. This includes your resume or curriculum vitae (CV), employment history (including job titles, employer names, dates of employment, and job responsibilities), educational background (including institutions attended, degrees obtained, fields of study, and graduation dates), professional skills and competencies, certifications and licenses, professional achievements and awards, portfolio links and work samples, and any other information you choose to include in your professional profile.

3.1.3 Job Preferences and Search Criteria

We collect information about your job search preferences, including desired job titles and roles, preferred industries and sectors, desired geographic locations (city, state, country, or remote work preferences), salary expectations and compensation requirements, employment type preferences (full-time, part-time, contract, freelance, internship), availability and start date preferences, willingness to relocate, and any other criteria you specify to filter or refine job recommendations.

3.1.4 Application-Specific Responses

When you use NextQuark to apply for jobs, we collect and store your responses to job-specific application questions. This may include cover letters, answers to screening questions, responses to diversity and equal opportunity questionnaires (provided on a voluntary basis), work authorization status, availability for interviews, references and referral information, and any other information you provide in connection with a specific job application.

3.1.5 Communication and Correspondence Data

If you contact us for support, provide feedback, or communicate with us through any channel (including in-app messaging, email, or social media), we collect the content of those communications, along with associated metadata such as timestamps, sender information, and any attachments.

When you access or use the Service, we automatically collect certain technical information about your device and connection. This includes your device type, model, and manufacturer; operating system type and version; app version and build number; unique device identifiers (such as IDFV on iOS); screen resolution and display metrics; language and locale settings; time zone; available storage and memory; and network connection type (Wi-Fi, cellular, etc.).

We collect data about how you interact with the Service, including jobs you view, save, bookmark, swipe on, or apply to; search queries and filters you use; features you access and how frequently; timestamps and duration of sessions; navigation paths and screens visited; interactions with notifications (opens, dismissals); application statuses and progress tracking; and logs of automation-related actions taken by the Service on your behalf.

3.2.3 Log and Diagnostic Data

We collect system log data and diagnostic information to maintain the stability, security, and performance of the Service. This includes IP addresses, crash reports, error logs, performance metrics, API call logs, and debugging data. 3.2.4 Location Data

We may collect coarse (approximate) location data derived from your IP address to provide location-relevant job recommendations. We may receive information about you from third-party sources in connection with the Service. 4. Legal Bases for Processing (GDPR Compliance)

For users located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or other jurisdictions where GDPR or equivalent data protection laws apply, we process your Personal Data only when we have a valid legal basis to do so. The legal bases we rely on include the following:

Consent: Where you have given explicit, informed consent for a specific processing purpose, such as connecting your email for application tracking, enabling push notifications, or allowing automated application features. You may withdraw consent at any time (see Section 9).

Performance of a Contract: Processing that is necessary to fulfill our obligations under the terms of service or agreement between you and NextQuark, such as creating and maintaining your account, processing job applications on your behalf, and providing core application assistance features.

Legitimate Interests: Processing that is necessary for our legitimate business interests, provided those interests are not overridden by your data protection rights. Legal Obligation: Processing that is necessary to comply with applicable legal obligations, such as responding to lawful requests from government authorities, complying with tax or regulatory requirements, or preserving data that may be relevant to legal proceedings.

Where required by applicable law (such as India’s DPDP Rules), we will notify you at least 48 hours before any automated deletion of your Personal Data upon expiration of the applicable retention period.
