What is a Tinder for jobs?

A Tinder for jobs is a job search app built around swiping. Instead of scrolling long lists and opening dozens of tabs, you look at one role at a time and decide in seconds. The idea works because most of the effort in a job search isn’t the decision. It’s the forms that come after it.

That is where Iris differs from a simple swipe feed. A right swipe doesn’t save the job for later. It starts the application.

How Iris works

  1. Add your resume. Iris reads it once to build your profile, so you don’t have to type it all out.
  2. Swipe through your matches. Each card shows the role, company, location and how well it fits you.
  3. Swipe right to apply. Iris completes the form on the company’s own job portal using your details.
  4. Track what happens next. Applications and recruiter replies stay together in one place.

Common questions

What does Tinder for jobs mean?

It describes a job search app where you swipe through job cards instead of scrolling through listings. In Iris, swiping right also sends the application, because AI fills in the form for you.

Is Iris free to try?

Yes. You can start for free with a set number of applications, and paid plans give you more.

Does swiping right really apply to the job?

Yes. Iris submits the application on the company’s own job portal using your resume and profile, and you can follow its status in the app.

Where can I use it?

On iPhone, Android and the web, all with the same account. You can also read more on the Iris blog.
