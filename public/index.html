Questions

Good to know.

What is the Tinder for jobs?

Iris is a Tinder for jobs: you swipe through job cards, and when you swipe right, AI fills in and submits the application for you on the company’s own job portal.

Is there a free plan?

Yes. You can start for free with a set number of applications and no card. Paid plans give you more, and you can cancel any time.

Does Iris really submit the application?

Yes. Applications go through the company’s own job portal, using your resume and profile. You can follow each one under Applications.

Will it apply to jobs I don’t want?

Swiping only applies to roles you swipe right on. Auto-apply only sends applications above the match score you set, and never to companies you block.

How does Iris find jobs?

Iris pulls live roles directly from company career pages and the applicant tracking systems behind them, then ranks them against your resume and preferences.

Which applicant tracking systems does Iris work with?

Roles come straight from systems including Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby and SmartRecruiters, and Iris’s agent also handles portals like Workday when a role links out to them.

How do I know Iris applied correctly?

Every application is logged with its status, and you can open it to see the details. Confirmation emails and recruiter replies land in your Iris inbox against the right application.

Will recruiters know I used Iris?

Your application arrives through the company’s normal portal, in your name, with your real details and experience.

Does Iris tailor my resume?

If you want it to. You choose Off, Honest or Aggressive, from your original resume to a stronger rewrite for each role, and you can change that any time.

I need visa sponsorship. Does Iris help?

Yes. You tell Iris your work authorization once, it uses that answer on applications, and you can filter for the roles and locations that work for you.

Why does Iris give me an inbox?

So replies don’t get lost. Confirmations, verification codes, recruiter emails and interview invites come to one place and stay attached to the right application.

Do I need the mobile app?

No. Iris works in your browser as well as on iPhone and Android, and your account is the same everywhere.
