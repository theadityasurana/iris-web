Questions

Good to know.

What is the Tinder for jobs?

Iris is a Tinder for jobs: you swipe through job cards, and when you swipe right, AI fills in and submits the application for you on the company’s own job portal.

Is Iris free?

Yes, you can start for free with a set number of applications. Paid plans give you more, and you can cancel any time.

Does Iris really submit the application?

Yes. Applications go through the company’s own job portal, using your resume and profile. You can follow each one under Applications.

Will it apply to jobs I don’t want?

Swiping only applies to roles you swipe right on. Auto-apply only sends applications above the match score you set, and never to companies you block.

Do I need the mobile app?

No. Iris works in your browser as well as on iPhone and Android, and your account is the same everywhere.
