About us

The fastest way to land your next role.

What we do

Applying shouldn’t be the hard part.

Applying for jobs today is slow, repetitive and unnecessarily frustrating. Candidates spend hours filling out the same forms across different portals, uploading resumes again and again, and navigating outdated systems.

Iris changes that. You discover opportunities and apply with a single swipe, while intelligent automation handles the heavy lifting behind the scenes.

What makes us different

Swipe to apply
