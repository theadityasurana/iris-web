A note from the founder

Why we built Iris.

I built Iris because job hunting shouldn’t feel like a full-time job.

Applying online is slow, repetitive and frustrating. You fill out the same forms on different portals and upload the same resume again and again, and the part that actually matters gets squeezed out.

We believe finding a job should be as seamless as discovering one. Our mission is to remove the friction, so you can focus on preparing for interviews, learning new skills and building a meaningful career.

Swipe right on what excites you, and we handle the rest.

Aditya SuranaFounder, Iris

Three things guide how we build it.

  1. 01

    Effortless by default

    A job search is exhausting enough. One swipe should be all it takes, wherever you are: phone or browser.

  2. 02

    You stay in control

    You pick the roles, set the match score and block the companies you don’t want. Every application is logged, so you can always see what was sent.

  3. 03

    Quality over volume

    No more spray-and-pray. One tailored application beats a hundred generic ones, so Iris focuses on roles that really fit you.
